## VPN troubleshooting | VPN
If VPN fails to connect, clear cached credentials, reset the VPN profile, and reinstall the client. For frequent drops on Wi-Fi, disable Wi-Fi power saving and switch the protocol to TCP.

## Account lockout policy | Password
Accounts lock after 5 failed attempts. Helpdesk can unlock in Active Directory. Enforce a password reset at next login after unlocking.

## Outlook sync issues | Email
For stuck syncing, recreate the Outlook profile and rebuild the OST file. If the mailbox is full, archive old mail or request a quota increase.

## Printer offline checklist | Printer
Restart the print spooler, confirm the printer has a static IP, and re-add the printer on the affected machines.

## Shared folder access | Access
Access requires manager approval. After approval, add the user to the matching AD security group.
